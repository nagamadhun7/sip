Sip Privacy Policy

Draft: October 21, 2026. Publisher name, contact details, public policy URL and server retention/deletion operations must be confirmed before publication.

Local tracking

Sip stores your profile, personal targets, situation preferences, caffeine plan, alcohol settings, drink records, saved recipes, containers, language settings and unsent notes in SQLite on your device. Local tracking requires no sign-in or subscription. We do not sell data, display advertising, or track you across other apps.

Barcode lookup

A requested lookup sends the barcode to Open Food Facts. If configured, a second lookup uses USDA FoodData Central through our authenticated server. Those services receive ordinary connection information. Barcode camera frames are not uploaded. Product data may be incomplete. Scanned drinks are cached locally. Label photographs remain in app storage for manual entry.

Typed descriptions, voice and AI

Common typed drinks match locally. Other accepted drink descriptions are sent only when you request a match. Voice uses your device speech recognition service; depending on your device and language, that service may process audio online under Apple or Google's policies. The resulting text uses the same local/server matching process. Unsent notes are not automatically uploaded.

Goal recipes send your request, size, dietary filters, remaining sugar and caffeine limits, bedtime, focus, age band and pregnancy/nursing or medical-plan flags to our server and OpenAI. Drink history and raw Health records are not sent. Do not include identifying or sensitive information in free text that is unnecessary for the request.

Drink photographs

Only when you tap Estimate drink, Sip sends the captured JPEG to our server and OpenAI to identify a likely drink and estimate its volume. You review or edit before saving. The model does not produce nutrition values. The server does not persist the photo; it caches a hash and the validated classification/portion result for up to 24 hours plus a daily cleanup interval. Photos can be inaccurate; non-drinks are not logged.

Accounts, caches and purchases

Server features create an anonymous Supabase session, whose tokens are stored securely on your device. The server uses the anonymous identifier for rate limits and recipe caches. Recipe results and hashed request/context keys remain for up to 30 days plus a daily cleanup interval. Usage counters remain to enforce limits. OpenAI requests disable response storage, but provider security/abuse retention can still apply. RevenueCat and the platform store process subscriptions and purchase restoration using the anonymous identifier. Sip does not receive your payment card number.

Health and notifications

With permission, Sip imports hydration from Apple Health or Health Connect and exports known logged fluid/nutrients. Raw Health records remain local and are not sold, used for advertising or sent to the AI service. You can disconnect and revoke access through device Health settings. Deleting Sip-written synced entries removes Sip's corresponding samples; records written by other apps are not deleted. Local notifications use your saved preferences and logs; no remote push service is required.

Crash reports

If crash reporting is configured, Sentry receives technical error types, stack traces and app version information to diagnose problems. We disable logs, breadcrumbs, replay and performance profiling, and remove user identity, request data and custom profile/note/photo context from JavaScript reports. We do not attach drink or Health records to diagnostics. The final publisher must configure the crash provider's retention policy and disclosure before enabling production collection.

Sharing, backups and deletion

CSV exports and PNG recipe cards are shared only to the destination you choose. Your operating system may include local app files in device backups. Settings → Your data → Delete all Sip data erases local records, drafts and preferences and cancels reminders. It does not cancel a store subscription, erase previously shared files, delete other apps' Health records or remove remote usage records. Manage subscriptions in your platform store. The publisher must provide a contact and a verified procedure for remote data deletion requests before release.

Service providers

Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, Supabase, OpenAI, RevenueCat, and Sentry have their own applicable policies. Regional processor arrangements and production retention must be confirmed by the publisher.
