Sip Privacy Policy

Draft updated October 20, 2026

Local drink tracking

Sip stores drink records, saved drinks, nutrition, targets and preferences in a local database on your device. No sign-in is required for local tracking. Sip does not upload your drink history and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Barcode lookup

If you choose to look up a beverage, Sip sends its barcode number to Open Food Facts. The service also receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Camera images are processed locally to detect barcodes and are not uploaded. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Drink maker and subscriptions

Lab runs offline. When you tap Generate in Goal, Sip creates an anonymous Supabase session and sends your entered request, remaining sugar/caffeine limits, bedtime, focus, age band, pregnancy/nursing status and dietary filters to the recipe server and OpenAI. Drink history is not sent. Tokens are stored in the device secure store. OpenAI requests use store:false; provider abuse monitoring retention may still apply. See OpenAI privacy and Supabase privacy.

The server retains recipes and hashed request/context cache keys for up to 30 days plus a daily cleanup interval. Usage counters are retained under the anonymous user ID to enforce limits. Pro uses RevenueCat and your platform store, with the anonymous ID, to process/verify purchases. See RevenueCat privacy. No advertising SDK is used.

Optional permissions

Camera access enables barcode detection. Notifications enable local drink reminders. Both are optional and can be changed in device settings. This release does not request microphone or Health data access.

Export and backups

CSV export creates a temporary file on your device and opens the operating system share sheet. You select the destination. Your device backup settings may include Sip data in system backups.

Deletion

Settings → Your data → Delete all Sip data erases the app's local history, saved drinks and preferences, and cancels scheduled reminders. Previously exported files and operating-system backups are managed separately by you. Local deletion does not delete the anonymous service identity or server usage records, and does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions through Apple or Google. The publisher must provide a remote account deletion/support process before enabling the public service.

Third-party data

Barcode nutrition is provided by Open Food Facts under its database license. Nutrition may be incomplete or inaccurate. Missing values are not assumed to be zero.

Contact

The publisher’s public support contact must be added before this policy is published and the app is submitted to stores.
